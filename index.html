Welcome to Prompt Paws: Cozy AI Cafe! 🥐🤖

What is this game? It's a cozy Tower-Defense game for AI Agent Architecture! Woodland critters send Pastry Orders (= User Prompts) down the conveyor belt, and you place Robot-Cat Chefs (= AI Models, Guardrails & RAG) on counter slots to bake them before the Pickup Window!

STEP 1 🧁 Pick an AI Chef

Click a station on the left menu (e.g. 🥐 Flash Oven = Fast LLM, 🛡️ Sieve = Guardrail, 🍓 Berry Patch = RAG).

STEP 2 ➕ Click a Map Slot

Click any glowing [➕ Slot] pad beside the conveyor belt to place your Robot-Cat Chef (click again to ⬆️ Upgrade!).

STEP 3 ▶️ Open Cafe!

Click ▶️ Open Cafe to start baking—or click ✨ Auto-Chef Blueprint to watch an optimal AI pipeline automatically!
